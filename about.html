I'm a security analyst who learns by breaking things — responsibly. Alongside my research, I've completed two cybersecurity internships — at IBM SkillsBuild and Redynox — applying OWASP Top 10 methodology, protocol-layer traffic analysis, and security automation. I'm a Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate holder, ranked in the top 9% on TryHackMe, and currently seeking a Security Analyst or Junior Penetration Tester role where proven vulnerability discovery translates into real impact.
